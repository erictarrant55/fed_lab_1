Story

Set 1000 years in the future from 2026, in the year 3026. Welcome to a dystopian world where humans are no longer around and every so-called "living being" is some kind of humanoid-like android.

However, hiding in the city which used to be known as Seattle, there is one last real human being.

The player in the story is this last human and is trying to survive from these androids who have eradicated the human race.
